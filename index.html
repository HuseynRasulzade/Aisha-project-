What is Sakina?

One calm app for your mind, free for everyone

Sakina brings daily mental self-care into one simple place. Log your mood, name your feelings with an emotion wheel, settle down with short breathing exercises, and listen to guided meditations when you need to slow down.

For deeper work there are thought-reframing exercises, gratitude notes, habit tracking, a planner and multi-week programs, all grouped into four easy hubs.

Your data stays encrypted, there are no ads, and you can begin as a guest without signing up. It installs like an app on phone or desktop and keeps working offline.

Please note: Sakina is a self-help tool and does not replace a licensed professional. In a crisis, contact your local emergency number.
