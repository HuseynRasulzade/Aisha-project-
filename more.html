Is it really free?

Yes. All tools are free, with no subscription and no ads.

Is my data private?

Data is stored encrypted, your journal can be locked, and nothing is sold or shared.

Can it replace therapy?

No. It supports daily self-care and works alongside professional help.

Do I need an account?

No. Use it as a guest, and create an account only if you want to sync devices.

Can I install it on my phone?

Yes. Use your browser's "Install app" or "Add to Home Screen" option.

Which methods does it use?

CBT, mindfulness, ACT, breathwork and standard questionnaires.
